Hivemind Enterprise

Hivemind Enterprise is an AI and autonomy development platform. Aircraft OEMs, defense primes, research organizations, and governments use Hivemind Enterprise to build autonomy for a specific vehicle platform and mission set (see videos and articles below). It has four parts.

Forge

Workflows to design, develop, train, test, and deploy AI pilots, including scenario generation and simulation at scale.

EdgeOS

The low-latency runtime on board every Hivemind-enabled aircraft and vessel, built for mission-critical robotics.

Pilot

A catalog of extensible autonomy blocks: sensing, world modeling, state estimation, planning and controls, and multi-agent teaming behaviors. Also includes reference architecture and reference implementation pilots.

Commander

Command-and-control interfaces connecting Hivemind-enabled systems to human operators.
